The Empyrean Pantheon is comprised of 6 gods, each of which hold dominion over one of the [[The Perpetual Alchemy|fundamental energies]] of the universe. They also hold patronage over an aspect of mortal life.

### Gomorrah, Goddess of Fire, Patron of War
===Great Primordial Fire Dragon, responsible for Fotiá, or the fuel of the Perpetual Alchemy. https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#brass%20greatwyrm_ftd ===

### Desmodus, God of Earth, Patron of Creation
===Great Primordial Earth Dragon, responsible for Gi, or the foundations of the Perpetual Alchemy. https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#copper%20greatwyrm_ftd ===

### Andrefana, Goddess of Air, Patron of Travel
===Great Primordial Storm Dragon, responsible for Aéras, or the mechanics of the Perpetual Alchemy (it's push and pull forces). https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#blue%20greatwyrm_ftd ===

### Aquias, God of Water, Patron of Family
===Great Primordial Ice Dragon, responsible for Neró, or the coolant of the Perpetual Alchemy https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#silver%20greatwyrm_ftd ===
### Temryava, Goddess of Darkness, Patron of Death
===Great Primordial Shadow Dragon, responsible for Skotádi, or the output of the Perpetual Alchemy. https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#black%20greatwyrm_ftd ===

### Halcyon, God of Light, Patron of Life
===Great Primordial Prismatic Dragon, responsible for Fos, or the input of the Perpetual Alchemy. https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#white%20greatwyrm_ftd ===

===Light is the element of Order, of stasis. a blanket of light means that everything has frozen. The Perpetual Alchemy takes the light of the greater void surrounding it, and transmutes it into Darkness.===

===Darkness is the element of Chaos, of the ever-changing. Mortals, by definition, are ever changing, as is their world. Thus, the Perpetual Alchemy creates darkness to form the mortal coil.===
