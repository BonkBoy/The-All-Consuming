The oldest still standing city of the [[Elf|Elves]], Canopy resides amongst the treetops of the Alterwood. Due to Elves being incredibly long lived, most of Canopy's original architecture has remained, with very few modern innovations being incorporated into the city itself. Long, wooden walkways allow for on-foot travel between the city's many buildings, and rope lifts allow for quick & easy access to & from the forest floor. Due to the nature of essentially being a floating city, much of it relies on the Elves' mastery of the Arcane.

Canopy is ruled over by High Shaman Sienna, selected from birth via a special ritual known only to those sworn in to the High Shaman's inner circle. The High Shaman will then rule the city for the whole of their natural life. The Elves of the city believe in Communal living as much as possible, so instead of being split into districts, the city is essentially one big organism. They also believe that communing directly with nature is one of the highest honours that can be bestowed on you, and since the gods are the essence of the natural order, their mightiest warriors commune with the gods to gain power, becoming Hexblades.
