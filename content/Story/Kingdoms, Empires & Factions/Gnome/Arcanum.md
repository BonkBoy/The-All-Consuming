An mixture of Ingenious problem solving and good old [[Gnome]] Cunning, Arcanum is currently the only Gnome kingdom. After gaining their freedom from servitude under the Dragonborn, the first free Gnomes managed to manoeuvre their way into securing a parcel of land with which was theirs to do what they wish with. The Gnomes immediately set to work, creating a sprawling metropolis with which to house their rapidly exploding population. Since they are masters of [[Runetronics]], the city itself is a technological marvel: self-suspending trams, small passenger vehicles, airships and the like are all common sights in Arcanum. Due to being enslaved once themselves, they are strictly against any kind of indentured servitude: there is no such thing as an employment contract in Arcanum. Instead, you are paid according to the quality and quantity of your work, and all manner of folk are free to live in Arcanum.

Arcanum is governed by a group of 7 magisters, collectively known as the Magisterium. They deliberate collectively on all matters, with a team of advisors representing each facet of the city helping them reach verdicts. This can however mean that social change is slow: everyone must agree before anything is passed.

Each facet of the city is represented by a guild which controls who can do what work: if your guild-assigned job is not to your liking, someone else takes the job and you are assigned somewhere else. Many of the guilds will work interchangeably with each other, and will often allow workers to flow between jobs of different kinds from different guilds.

Guild of Runetronics - Arcanum's Research guild, In charge of Runetronic R&D, as well as supply & distribution of [[Aretonic Crystals]].
Guild of Agriculture - Arcanum's farming guild, In charge of all things Agriculture, including distribution of Runetronic farming technology as well as supply & distribution of food.
Guild of Warfare - Arcanum's defense guild, armed with the latest in Runetronic technology.
Guild of Commerce - Arcanum's banking guild, who control the flow of gold into & out of the kingdom. Most of Arcanum's monetary assets are stored in this guild's banks.
Guild of Welfare - Arcanum's citizen support guild: responsible for constructing new housing, housing new & old citizens alike, & generally making sure everyone can at least survive.
