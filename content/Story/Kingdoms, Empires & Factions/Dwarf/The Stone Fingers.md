A group of 8 settlements carved into the sides of the Talon mountains that the [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] call their homes. They are expansions on the original tribal villages that the Dwarves founded, having expanded by tunnelling into the sides of the mountains. Each settlement is connected by an intricate network of stone bridges and walkways.

Each settlement is governed individually by an elected "Elder", which form to make a Conclave that confers on decisions that affect the entire group. Each settlement is self sufficient, but they each specialise in some aspect of Dwarven culture.

Stonemason's Peak - Master stonemasons & builders hail from this village.
Cracked Precipice - Home of the Conclave
Foundation - Tanners & Leatherworkers
Crucible - Home of hunters & warriors
Crystalhome - Home of herbalists & alchemists
Leyway - Home of artificers & arcanists
Overhang - Home of merchants & traders
Bouldertown - General residences

Notable Institutions:
Skylight - Conclave Meeting Hall
Stund Avor Okumudov - Institute of higher learning
Hammer & Chisel - Stonemason's Workshop
The Jumping Goat - popular tavern within Overhang
