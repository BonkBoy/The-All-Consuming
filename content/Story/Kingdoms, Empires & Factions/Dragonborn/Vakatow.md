The former capital of the [[Scaleshorn Empire]], Vakatow is a glittering Jewel of a city, showing just how much control the [[Dragonborn]] have over the natural world. Spires of pure marble, glittering with magic. Streets lined with Arcane sigils to help speed up transport. All manner of markets, institutions, housing, all whilst patrolled and protected by the venerated Clawguard. Vakatow is also home to the Autarch, the ruler of the Dragonborn and his many advisors.

Vakatow was built on the foundations of the original 6 Dragonborn clans: The Fotiá, the Gi, the Aéras, the Neró, the Skotádi, and the Fos. These clans now comprise the 6 noble houses of Vakatow of the same names. All Dragonborn within the city can claim membership to these houses, however cousins of cousins of cousins of the like hold no real standing: it's more to determine what role they fulfill in the city. Members of other races can be admitted into these houses, however they require a sponsor of sufficient standing within the house, and the acceptance of the house Archon to join the house.

House Fotiá are a warrior caste: most of the great generals of the Scaleshorn Empire hail from this house, and since the empire's dissolution, most of the Guard captains & commanders hail form this house as well.

House Gi are the builders and craftspeople of Vakatow. They are responsible for the glittering spires that adorn the city, and have gone out of their way to make the city look as beautiful as possible. They also man the city's armouries, crafting fine arms and armour for the guard and for those with the money to splash.

House Aéras are the city's innovators: the scientists and engineers. Every innovation that has come from Vakatow has its roots in their laboratories. Every day, they push the boundary of what is possible, and are also responsible for the ingenious mixing of technology & magic.

House Neró are the city's healers: whether that be a doctor, a chirurgeon, an alchemist, a psychiatrist or anything in between. They are responsible for the health and wellbeing of the citizens of Vakatow.

House Skotádi are the city's pious people: clerics, vicars, priests, morticians, shamans, anything with a holy or spiritual bent (even paladins). They staff the temples & places of worship in Vakatow, regardless of what that worship entails.

House Fos are the city's leaders: the city's very first Autarch belonged to house Fos, and every Autarch since belongs to house Fos. Most council members also belong to this house, though high ranking individuals from other house are on the council to ensure everyone is represented.

The city is divided into 5 districts: The Crown (Government District), Breezetop (Noble district), Magmastone (Commercial District), Haven (Residential District), Bedrock's Shield (Military District)

Notable Institutions:
Maw of the World - Palace of the Autarch
Vakatow Academy - Institute of higher learning, however need to be a member of a noble house to enter so very rare to see non-Dragonborn here.
Crystalback Forges - A huge conglomerate of workshops, where most of the city's craftspeople ply their trade.
The Perch-on-high - popular tavern within Magmastone, something of a cultural melting pot within the city.
Dragon's Speartip - Garrison of the Clawguard
