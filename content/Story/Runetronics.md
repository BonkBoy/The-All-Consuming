Runetronics is the art of manipulating the magical energies of Aretina through rune-engraved [[Aretonic Crystals]] to reproduce a singular natural phenomenon. For example, you may inscribe a rune of air onto an Aretonic Crystal, then strike it with a lightning spell to allow it to store the electrical charge, which can then be discharged at will. Multiple crystals of different types can be combined with other conductive materials to form a Runetronic circuit, which can cause more complex phenomena.
