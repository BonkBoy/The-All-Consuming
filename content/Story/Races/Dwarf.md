The Dwarves hail from the Talon Mountains, where they chiselled their existence into the very stone around them. They primarily subsisted on the rich bounty of mountain goats that would graze halfway to the peaks of the Talon Mountains, but also understood how perilous their lives were, leading to a deep connection with spirits they believed inhabited the mountains. The disparate villages would unite as one when the [[Dragonborn]] discovered their existence whilst spreading across Lucan, forming something of a tribal council to defend their homes. The war was short lived, as the Dwarves were unused to combat like this, even with the homefield advantage, and remained subjugated until the dissolution of the [[Scaleshorn Empire]]. Dwarves are incredibly skilled craftspeople, and very sturdy, excelling in close quarters combat.
