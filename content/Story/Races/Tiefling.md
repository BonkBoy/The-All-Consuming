Tieflings are [[Human|Humans]] who have been blessed (or Cursed) by one of the [[Empyrean Pantheon|six gods]] at some point in their lineage. For whatever reason, the magic of a god would seep into the womb, twisting the Human baby into a Tiefling. Whether this is a blessing or a curse depends on the culture that the Tiefling is being born into: some Kingdoms venerate Tieflings, whereas others are quietly shunned. Whichever god decides to bless the Tiefling bestows some of their domain onto them: making them particularly proficient in things to do with that domain.
