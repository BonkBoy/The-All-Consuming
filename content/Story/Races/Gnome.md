Gnomes are actually the result of Arcane experimentation gone too far. Ancient [[Dragonborn]] mages, during the nascent days of the [[Scaleshorn Empire]], meddled with forces beyond their ken in an attempt to make Flesh simulacrums that could be used for all manners of work. However, in attempting to give these simulacrums more autonomy, they gradually developed sentience. The wider Empire learned of this, and knowing that the political outcry would devastate the empire during it's early days of conquest, the mages responsible were put to death and the Gnomes released from servitude. Naturally apt at magic, and having developed a keen sense of cunning due to the work they were made to do, are very intelligent creatures and some of the foremost leaders in research into the natural world and in [[Runetronics]]. They hold no ill will towards their creators, so long as they are allowed their freedom.
