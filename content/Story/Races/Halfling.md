Halflings are the offspring of [[Gnome|Gnomes]] & [[Elf|Elves]]. When the Gnomes gained their freedom, they set up their own Sovereignties throughout the Empire, which encouraged migration of the other races. As it turned out, Gnomes & Elves were genetically compatible, which led to the creation of Halflings. Most Halflings are scholars or mages, owing to the strong magic in their bloodlines, though some prefer a more hands on (sneaky) approach.
