The Tabaxi are, like Orcs, native to the plains of Starmold. Most Tabaxi however were not nomadic: many lived in small, disparate settlements across the continent. When the Scaleshorn began their colonisation of Starmold, many of these settlements were wiped out or dissolved, leading to quite a bit of resentment towards the Scaleshorn. Due to this, many Tabaxi were forcibly rehomed, with many being shipped back to start new lives on Lucan. Almost all of the ancestral homes of the Tabaxi were wiped out in the cataclysm, and the new age Tabaxi have no recollection or knowledge of their birthplace.
