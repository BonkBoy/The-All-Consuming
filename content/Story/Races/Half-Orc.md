Half-Orcs trace their lineages to the plains of Starmold, before the cataclysm devastated the continent. The original Orcs lived as nomads, traversing the once large plains & living off of the land as they went. When the [[Scaleshorn Empire]] originally colonised the continent, most of the Orcs would fight back against the invaders, but some would turn against their brethren in hopes of a better life. Gradually, those Orcs that turned would fade from existence as they intermingled with the myriad races of the Empire. Then, the cataclysm struck, wiping almost all life from the surface of Starmold, making the Orcs extinct. This makes Half-Orcs quite the rare sight indeed.
