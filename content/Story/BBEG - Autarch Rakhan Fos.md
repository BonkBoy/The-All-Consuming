Autarch Rakhan Fos is determined to see the Dragonborn people survive. His best scholars and researchers have all confirmed the same thing: in the next 50 years, all life on Lucan is destined to be consumed. He does not know why, or how, but not even the gods themselves will stop him from saving his people. He has installed a puppet body-double to act in his stead as he takes a hands on approach.
