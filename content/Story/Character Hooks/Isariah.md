Isariah has spent the last few years doing all he can to forget: reliant on an Amnesiac Elixir, brewed from Enchanted Spring Water, Canopy sprigs & powder of mistletoe berries, to prevent his memories from crippling his mind, Isariah latches onto the one thing he allows himself to remember: the Shallow Reavers must burn. He is employed by a faceless organisation to do just that: in turn he is paid in shipments of these elixirs. He is getting close to unravelling the foundations of the reavers: he can feel it deep in his soul.

Elixir of Amnesia:

1. Add 2 drops of Canopy Spring Water to your cauldron
2. Gently heat for 20 seconds
3. Add 2 Dragonheart sprigs to your cauldron
4. Stir 3 times, clockwise
5. Cast an Illusion cantrip on the cauldron
6. Leave to brew and return in 45-60 minutes
7. Add 2 measures of 80 Proof alcohol to the mortar
8. Add 4 mistletoe berries to the mortar
9. Crush into a medium-fine powder using the pestle
10. Add 2 pinches of the crushed mixture to your cauldron
11. Stir 5 times, anti-clockwise
12. Cast an Enchantment Cantrip on the cauldron to complete the potion

===The "organisation" he is employed by is actually Commodore Flotsam: she wishes to regain control of her city after she failed to stop the Reavers from becoming so entrenched. The Reavers themselves are responsible for several travesties that have occurred in recent years, the least being the Haven Butcher, Isariah himself. The Reavers, responsible for the Desecration of The Freehold, are harbingers of the cycle, working under the will of Temryava and led by Palar Shiemon, who is unknown to most actually a powerful necromancer. They are essentially trying to raise an undead army with which to fell the Human kingdoms.===
