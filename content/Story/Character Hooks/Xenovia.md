Xenovia, like Amber, was once a student of the Lodestone University. She dedicated her academic career to the study of the occult: old, ancient "gods" and fell beings that sought to claim dominion over the world in times past. She would undertake expeditions into ancient ruins maligned by evil energies for her work, and through this became familiar with strange magics the likes have never been seen before, becoming foremost in her field. She communed with old scholars who had attempted to do the same to understand what she had found. Because of this, the Magisterium requested her expertise on their research initiative.

===Xenovia doesn't have much of a background hook, so we'll make one. These weird and wonderful magics, these malign energies: they are actually evidence of planes of existence outside of this one. Planeswalkers & astral travellers who came across this little pocket universe attempting to enter or exert control of the Perpetual Alchemy would leave these residual energies behind, which would twist the landscapes they arrived in. Xenovia could be the key to cracking open the Great Machine.===
