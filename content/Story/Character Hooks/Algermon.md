Algermon, a priest of the Church of the Final Dawn, pays his penance to the church after they saved his life 5 years ago. Under the tutelage of Azuuhar, one of the grand clerics, he has become a devout follower of Halcyon, with the mission of spreading his word and his love across the continent. Algermon is tasked with escorting Amber & Xenovia to Levashan so that they may conduct an experiment into long-term preservation of food. What is seemingly a simple task, however, will unweave a web of intrigue at the highest levels of society.

===Basic premise is that certain elements in government have realised the cycle that takes place in the Perpetual Alchemy every 10,000 years, and instead of trying to stop or prevent it, they want to move themselves away from Lucan to Balkan to build a utopia consisting of an "elite" class only. Azuuhar has infiltrated this inner circle, and plans on either dismantling it or trying to save as many people as he can the same way, and Algermon is an unwitting pawn in his plan.
