upbringing:  
Grew up on a small farm with Mother, not rich but not poor, no siblings. Part of a large, tight knit community where everyone knew eachother. Enjoyed playing in the dirt, had an immediate interest in plants, growing things, doing farm work, working hard to help provide meals/furs/tools for the community etc.  
  
During Kali's late teens things changed, the land was taken over by (some kind if corrupt government thing, seeking to conquer as much land as possible, has taken over many villages) enforcing strict rules, curfews, mandatory work, limited food provision, limited freedom. Would use force against Kali's friends and family, prison for small things. Kali's community had to steal food, tools and provisions just to get by, if caught they would be locked away/tortured/killed.  
  
defining event:  
Kali couldn't stand seeing the community that was once so full of love and joy be reduced to sad, poor, skin and bones. Her mother died after a hard beating for stealing food. With her main connection gone, she formed a group (???) where she taught anyone willing how to steal, fight, escape the land and gain their freedom (maybe sneaking people out using her carriage under guise of transporting goods). This carried on for a couple of years until a small uprising occured and she was discovered as the leader, and she had to flee in order to save her life. She travels, trying to find a way to save her old home and anyone left.

Bounces around from area to area, helping people when needed.
