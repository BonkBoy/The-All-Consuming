Amber, a bright young graduate of Lodestone University, has been commissioned by the Magisterium of Arcanum to create a method to preserve food for a long period of time (think long voyages over the ocean). Traditional methods, like salting meats or taking non-perishables, just won't cut it: They need to be able to take anything and everything you could think of. Amber & her friend Xenovia are in charge of making this happen, being given essentially a blank check.

Her old friend, Nyxara, comes calling, apoplectic to see that Amber is still living her lie. She is now working with the Reavers as one of their chief scientists, and launches an attack on the labs.

===I see this going one of 3 ways: Using glyphs of warding with ice spells to cool a room to below freezing, some kind of temporal lock so that the food is preserved in the state in time that it was placed in the temporal lock, or creating some kind of portable portal that the food can be moved through. We'll do this via ability checks: 10 successes on a certain method means the method was a success, but 5 failures blows the whole thing and they have to try something else. Try and get them to incorporate Runetronics. (Arcane Engineering book will help with this)===
