A timeline of notable recorded events.

0 CE:
The start of recorded history, as the first [[Dragonborn]] settlements created the art of writing.

40 CE:
The Dragonborn settlements begin to form into different clans based on family ties.

45 CE:
Certain clans begin forming larger group settlements by exchanging family members. Small conflicts ensue as not all of the exchanges lead to compatible pairings.
The shift towards agriculture begins: hunter gatherer communities become farming communities, and the less dangerous lifestyle and easier availability of food causes a population boom for the Dragonborn.

69 CE:
10 great clans form: food & labour trade between disparate settlements is booming.

101 CE:
4 of the great clans begin a fight for power: they wish to control and ensure their clan's futures. The 6 other clans form a coalition led by clan Fos to prevent the conflict from destroying all they've built so far.

105 CE:
The 4 rebelling clans are crushed. The coalition of clans that emerged victorious decide to stay united, merging their settlements into 1, creating the first city. This will be the basis for [[Vakatow]].

107 CE:
The head of clan Fos, Lustar Fos, is named the Autarch of the burgeoning city, destined to lead it to greatness. The clans are renamed to houses.

130 CE:
Some Dragonborn begin displaying latent magical abilities.

135 CE:
A freak outburst of magic destroys half of the connected Dragonborn settlements. The sudden influx of magical energy awakens the latent magical potential present in all Dragonborn. The Autarch decrees that the city will be rebuilt, bigger and better, and renamed Vakatow.

535 CE:
400 years of peace and prosperity reign for Vakatow. However, space and resources have become an issue: Vakatow is bursting at the seams with people. House Fotiá hits upon the brilliant idea of expanding their territories outwards, into new lands. The Autrach commands a mobilisation of the city's hunters & guards into an army. 

536 CE:
The army of Vakatow, led by Autarch Kaardastial Fos, discovers the small farming settlements of the Humans to the south. Whilst they put up some resistance, the farmers are easily crushed by the might of the army. To celebrate this first victory in their conquest, the houses decree that the [[Scaleshorn Empire]] is born.

539 CE:
The Scaleshorn Empire continues spreading south, reaching the Talon mountains. Here they discover the Dwarves, living in disparate settlements known as [[The Stone Fingers]]. Again, they crush any form of resistance, and conscript the Dwarves' master craftsmen into their servitude.
