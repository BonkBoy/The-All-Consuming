Progology is a religion based around the study of the natural world. [[Gnome|Gnomes]], having originally been exposed to the faith of their creators the [[Dragonborn]], decided to take a markedly different & hands on approach to their worship of the Pantheon. Progology is essentially the pursuit of knowledge, and so Progology reveres the goddess [[Empyrean Pantheon|Andrefana]]. The High Scholar of Runescribe University is also the head of the Church of Progology.

They depict Andrefana as a great guiding star in the night sky.
