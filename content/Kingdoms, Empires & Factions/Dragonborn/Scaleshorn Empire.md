The now dissolved Scaleshorn Empire reigned for thousands of years, from the southernmost tip of Lucan to the easterly shores of Starmold. The might of the [[Dragonborn]] allowed them to conquer and rule over all other races. the Scaleshorn Empire are responsible for most of the technological advancements in the world today, as they were obsessed with understanding and manipulating the modern world. The capital, [[Vakatow]], remains the seat of power of the Dragonborn to this day, a shining Jewel of Arcane mastery and good old fashioned ingenuity. A majority of people who live in Scaleshorn Empire holdings did not know poverty: whilst they were asked to provide their fair share, they were in turn provided with everything they could ever need or want.

Those who existed outside these though, in subjugated kingdoms or far off villages, were not bestowed with such luxuries. The Empire turned the screws on these places, milking them for all of their worth. It is of no surprise then that when their main forces were decimated during the cataclysm, the Empire dissolved, returning land & cities to their original owners, hoping to spare themselves from the intense backlash that awaited them now they were weakened.

Sects of Dragonborn to this day relish a return to their days of dominance, and wish to restore the Empire to it's former glory. So far, these are mostly isolated cells, however the Autarch of Vakatow worries for what lays on the horizon.
