Levashan is a relatively new port city built on the edge of the Alterwood built by outcasts and renegades from [[Canopy]], those who no longer believe in bending to the world's every whim. Levashan welcomes innovation with open arms, along with every risk and danger that comes with it. Many an unscrupulous character calls Levashan home, from mad geniuses and scientists who's research was outlawed to the lowest brigands who would kill you for a copper piece. The city is split into 4 districts, with the east of the city being dominated by the Port of Levashan, the north being dominated by the Belltower District, the west being dominated by the Gold Quarter, and the South being dominated by Cattown. Levashan is ruled by Commodore Flotsam in a strict meritocracy: she says she is the most capable to rule, so she rules, and anyone is free to try and prove her wrong, if they survive facing her.

Port of Levashan - Levashan's primary trade route in & out of the city: travelling by sea allows traders to avoid the dangers of the forest. Also home to the mercenary group [[Shallow Reavers]], tasked by Commodore Flotsam to police Levashan.
Belltower District - Named for the freestanding belltower within the middle of the district, this is where most of the city's "innovation" comes from. Any class of intellectual is allowed to conduct unimpeded research here, as long as they pay fealty to the Commodore.
Gold Quarter - Home of the city's "nobility". Trade barons, pirate captains, slave traders and many more make their home here: as long as you have the coin and the power. Commodore Flotsam also rules from here.
Cattown - The slums of Levashan. Most of the city's brigands and cutthroats hail from here, and the district is a hodgepodge of derelict buildings and shanty towns.
