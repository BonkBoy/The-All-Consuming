A large fortress city originally constructed by the [[Scaleshorn Empire]], it was captured by the Humans after a swift & bloody siege during the [[quartz/content/History|Splintering]]. It is now the centre of the Human world, with the largest Human density of any city. The Ironbaron family have ruled as a Monarchy since the city was captured, and the surrounding lands of [[The Freehold]] pay fealty to King Edward Ironbaron. Whilst some humans are capable of magic, most are magically inept, and thus rely on mundane ingenuity & strength through engineering & mechanics instead, whilst making the most of [[Runetronics|Runetronic]] inventions acquired from their allies in [[Arcanum]].

Everyone in Bastion answers to the Crown: the monarch's word is law, and all taxes go straight into the royal treasury. Bastion's upper class consists of several noble families, all of whom were the main conspirators of the siege during the Splintering. These are: the Minerva family, the Steelock family, the Pious Family, the Lochland family, the Erskine family, the Diedrich family and the Katrina family. The lower class consists of the descendants of those who fought in the Splintering siege, as well as descendants of the survivors of that siege.

Trade companies rule the roost when it comes to Bastion's economy: they are Bastion's biggest source of income. They are responsible for any home manufactured goods, as well as foreign goods imported from other kingdoms. These are the big 4, royally endorsed by the king:

United Mercantile Front - Traders of exotic goods from foreign lands
Hardsteel Mining - Traders of ores & ingots of all kinds, also dabble in local blacksmiths, armorers & jewellers.
Farmhands Inc. - Traders of food & crops.
Lodestone - Scientific research development company, responsible for importing Runetronics.
