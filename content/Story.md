The light of the rising sun trickles in through the ornate stained glass window, illuminating the battered body of another wounded soldier. A warming glow emits from Algermon's hand as he watches the soldier's broken skin stitch itself back together, enraptured.

A firm hand falls on Algermon's shoulder, snapping him back to the moment. "It's time for us to move on, my boy", a firm but gentle voice says. Algermon turns to regard the voice: a priest, robed in the customary dress of apostles of the [[Church of the Final Dawn]], towers over him, with a hand held out in offering. Algermon takes his hand, and is quickly pulled to his feet. The priest, Azuuhar, beckons for Algermon to follow him out into the sunlight.

As they reach the top of the chapel's steps, Algermon spots a strange gaggle of faces further down the steps. Azuuhar stops, and leans in: "we've been ordered to join some sort of outreach program. We're to escort a group of academics to join some kind of joint research initiative the Magisterium has created. Each of us has been assigned a pair to escort." He points to two Tieflings in particular: "you're in charge of those two, and you're bound for [[Levashan]]." He grabs Algermon by the shoulders, and whispers "remember your oaths, and protect them with your life whilst they are in your care." He presses a parchment into Algermon's hand stamped with a prismatic wyvern, which lays out the orders for Algermon.

"By decree of the Autarch, you are charged with ensuring the safe passage of Amber Willow & Xenovia Horis to the port of Levashan and to the learning institution of Watersod Laboratories.

Find the Hook-Line-and-Sinker inn, and ask for Isariah.

May the light guide your path."

After making the trek to Levashan along the Wingways, Algermon, Amber & Xenovia find their way into the Hook-Line-and-Sinker. Algermon links up with Isariah, their escort whilst they are in the city, hoping to find the laboratories as quickly as possible. The local "authorities" interrupt the meeting, and Isariah strikes first, looking for the upper hand. The group wipe out 10 [[Shallow Reavers]], whilst Amber burns down the tavern. Kali, a local patron, decides to help out with their fight, and gives them a place to lie low, leading them to Astora's shack. After letting the heat die down, Isariah and Quiet navigate the back alleys of Levashan, avoiding attention as they make their way to the Belltower district.

After locating the laboratories, Amber & Xenovia discover their task: food preservation over long periods of time, with a huge gold sum being promised in return for a workable solution. Isariah meanwhile is tasked with the murder of a Shallow Reaver known as Farrow.

Amber & Xenovia immediately jump into the conundrum before them: Using the power of runetronics, they're going to explore 2 avenues. 1, they're going to try and keep a space constantly frozen using Ice spells, or 2, they're going to try and stop time in an area to prevent food from rotting. Amber begins tinkering with various circuits and [[Aretonic Crystals]], trying to formulate the beginnings of their design. Meanwhile, noticing the lack of provisions stocked in the Lab, Xenovia, Quiet & Kali go on a shopping trip, whilst Algermon checks in with the local Church of the Final Dawn temple, receiving orders from Azuuhar to keep watch over the girls. 

Whilst on their shopping trip, Quiet gets robbed, but catches the aspiring thief. It turns out to be a trap however, as the 2 Reavers that come to investigate the situation attempt to kill Quiet & Kali. Barely escaping with their lives, they make it back to the laboratory, and resolve to get the Reavers off their backs. After some rest, they make a daring heist on the Levashan Administrative Hub to steal the floor-plans to the Otliasar Estate, which is where the Reavers have set up shop. Not all goes according to plan, however, as Quiet destroys the record room, but luckily Algermon saves the day and grabs the records, leaving Jebediah to clean up the mess.

The group hunted down a Lieutenant Fallowfoot of the Reavers. They track him to a brothel that had been taken over as a base of operations. They bust in and kill that mofo. They haven't done this yet, but they will find stacks of papers outlining how they've massively increased their Aretonic crystal smuggling operation, and it stretches across the continent. They will also see a mention of a "Harbinger", but no mention of who this mysterious figure is, but they do know he's a benefactor of the Reavers with some serious pull on the world stage, as he's allowing them to operate freely.

The Dick Twisters take some much needed downtime after recent events, when Isariah receives a letter through the door detailing his next dead drop. Him & Quiet go to collect the dead drop, but find that someone has stole it. Isariah crashes out & finds a Shallow Reaver grunt, torturing him for the location of his boss, Lucius. They rendezvous with the rest of the group and head to the Gold Quarter Tavern to find him. Xenovia works the crowd of Reavers present at the bar, managing to woo one of them into bringing them Lucius. Amber then uses a Mage Hand to steal his coin purse, where Algermon, Kali & Isariah are waiting to drop a barrel on his head. They succeed, rolling him back to the labs, where they start interrogating him for the location of the stolen dead drop & info on who the Harbinger is.
