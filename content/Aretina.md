The world of Aretina is lush and Verdant, brimming with magic and good old Ingenuity both. Races & people of all ilk call Aretina their home, split among the 3 continents: Starmold to the north, Lucan to the Southwest, & Balkan to the Southeast.

Starmold acquired its name when, at some point in the distant past, splinters of a great comet impacted along the edges of the landmass, leaving great rips along the coastline, wiping out most of the life on the continent. Only the hardiest of species survived the cataclysm, and it took a thousand years for the continent to even become explorable again. Since then, a sect of Dragonborn from Lucan have recolonised the continent, and many burgeoning kingdoms have sprouted in the wake of the colonisation efforts.

Lucan can be seen as the cradle of civilisation: the earliest recorded history takes place within Lucan, and starts with the founding of the first Dragonborn city: [[Vakatow]]. For millennia, the Dragonborn ruled over Lucan as the [[Scaleshorn Empire]], spreading as far as Starmold in their bid to tame the world. However, since the cataclysm of Starmold, the Dragonborn hold on Lucan has weakened, and kingdoms controlled by other races have vied for power on the continent. The Scaleshorn, knowing the inevitable, gave up much of their territory in exchange for peaceful co-existence with the other races.

Balkan stands as a mostly unknown, hostile frontier. Several expeditions launched from both Starmold & Lucan disappeared, and no lines of communication between Balkan and the other two continents have remained open.
