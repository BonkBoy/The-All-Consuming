Half-Elves are a result of the expansion of the [[Scaleshorn Empire]]. Having conquered existing [[Elf|Elven]] and [[Human]] lands, the Empire would slowly allow migration of the peoples of these lands back into the heart of the Empire, allowing the races to mingle & breed. Half-Elves get some of the best of both worlds, having much longer lifespans than humans as well as their versatility and flexibility. Half-Elves have become such a common sight that it's no longer deemed strange to see the offspring of a human & an elf.
