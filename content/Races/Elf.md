Elves trace their ancestry back to the Forests of the Alterwood, and it's connected coastline. All Elves can boast a deep connection with Nature, and as such are very in-tune with the energies that flow within Aretina, making them naturally proficient in arcane matters. When the [[Dragonborn]] extended their conquest to the forests and the seas, they found the Elves to be quite Amicable, so absorbed them into their empire, exploiting their knack for the arcane to help with their war efforts. However, in recent times, with the development of Arcane Technology, something of a rift has formed within the Elven kingdoms: those that wish to preserve ancient ways and keep in touch with the natural balance of the world, and those that wish to use their arcane know-how to create bigger and better technological marvels.
