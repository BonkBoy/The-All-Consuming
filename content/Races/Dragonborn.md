The Progenitor race. The first race to assemble what we call Civilisation, and to then bring it to the other disparate tribes of other races. They are fiercely proud, and believe themselves to be Aretina's protectors. All Dragonborn can trace their lineage back to the original tribes who founded [[Vakatow]], though in more recent times, with the [[Scaleshorn Empire]] being dissolved, recent generations can claim to have lineages of other races. Due to a Dragon's innate magical potential, Dragonborn rank among the most potent magical users in the world, but most often focus on martial prowess, using their innate magical abilities as a supplement to rigorous battle training.

===It's of no surprise that the Dragonborn were the world's rulers. They are directly linked to the [[Empyrean Pantheon]], believed even to be direct descendants of their strongest champions during their cycle of razing the world.===
